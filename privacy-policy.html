Rollverse Privacy Policy

Rollverse (com.sandeep.rollverse) does not collect, store on servers, or share any personal information in this version. Game progress (coins, levels, settings) is saved only on your device.

The game may use your phone's motion sensors for tilt control and the vibration motor for feedback; this data never leaves your device.

Future versions may add advertising and in-app purchases through Google AdMob and Google Play Billing. If so, this policy will be updated first, ads will be non-personalised, and purchases will be protected by a parental gate.
